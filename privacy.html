Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the restricted Local Video Publisher B1 Test sandbox prototype.

Test environment

The sandbox is intended for controlled feasibility testing by authorized test users only.

Authorization data

The prototype may process OAuth authorization data required to connect an authorized test account to a supported platform. Credentials and access tokens must not be published on this website or committed to the public source repository used to host these pages.

Media and account data

Only the minimum account information and user-selected test media needed to exercise an approved sandbox integration should be processed.

Sharing

The sandbox is not intended to sell personal data or provide account credentials to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Retention

Sandbox evidence should avoid raw secrets. Test authorization material should be removed or revoked when no longer needed for the feasibility test.

Production policy

A production privacy policy will be finalized before any public release or production submission.
